## Points to check before using this letter
- If the trademark has been or will be assigned to a company, the **company** should issue the letter on its letterhead instead of the individual.
- The SKU list must match the product names and EAN codes that will appear on the listings.
- Some platforms provide their own template. If so, use it instead of this draft.
